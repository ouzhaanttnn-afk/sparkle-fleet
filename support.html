Common questions

Did I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device automatically. As long as you keep the game installed, you continue where you left off.

I can't reach a spot.

If your tool's reach is too short, the screen says "Too far". Step closer or buy a longer tool in the workshop.

How do I move the robot arm?

Walk up to the arm and press the use button, walk to the new spot and press it again.

What happens after the last ship?

When all four ships are done, harbour contracts open: levels get dirty again and every job pays more.
